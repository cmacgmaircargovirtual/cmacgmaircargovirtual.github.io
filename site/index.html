01About

Long-haul freight, the French way.

CMA CGM AIR CARGO is the air freight arm of the CMA CGM group, based at Paris-Charles de Gaulle. Its widebody freighters link Europe with the factories of Asia and the markets of the Americas. We fly that network in the simulator, rotation by rotation.

Airports 17

on three continents: Europe, Asia and the Americas.

Longest leg 15h+

Shanghai to Mexico City, across the Pacific.

A real freight network

Paris to Shanghai, Hong Kong, Hanoi and Campinas; Brussels, Liège and Madrid to China; a transpacific operation out of Chicago.

Pallets, not passengers

The 777F lifts up to 102 tonnes, the A330-200P2F covers the mid-size flows. Main-deck freighters only.

Long haul, multi-leg

Legs from 1½ to more than 15 hours, and rotations that chain three sectors. Plan fuel, payload and rest.

Tracked automatically

Newsky's free app logs the flight, files the PIREP and rates it. Every load earns revenue in a shared economy.

      06FAQ

      Before you close the doors.

      Anything else? Ask the crew on Discord.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. CMA CGM Air Cargo Virtual is a non-profit hobby project and Newsky is free to use.

      Which simulators can I use?

      Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024, X-Plane 11 and 12. The Newsky app runs on Windows.

      Which aircraft do I need?

      A Boeing 777 Freighter that Newsky recognises as a B77L, or an Airbus A330-200 freighter recognised as an A332. Payware or freeware, as long as Newsky detects it.

      Do I have to fly the 15-hour sectors?

      No. Every leg is a flight of its own. Start with the short Asian legs, such as Seoul to Wuxi or Zhengzhou, and move on to the long hauls when you are ready.

      Where do the routes and flight numbers come from?

      From the network published by CMA CGM AIR CARGO. Not every flight number is confirmed yet: they are added as we go and show as TBA until then.

      Do I have to fly on VATSIM or IVAO?

      No. Online networks are welcome but never required.

      What happens if my simulator crashes mid-flight?

      Newsky detects interrupted flights. Let a staff member know on Discord and we'll sort it out.

      Are you the real CMA CGM AIR CARGO?

      No. CMA CGM Air Cargo Virtual is a flight simulation community. We are not affiliated with the CMA CGM group, and nothing on this site is a real flight, a real booking or a real shipment.
